## Country boundaries

Country map silhouettes are generated from Natural Earth Admin 0 – Countries
vector data. Natural Earth raster and vector map data is in the public domain:
<https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/>.

Made with Natural Earth.

## Country names and metadata

Country names are localized with `i18n-iso-countries` (MIT License), and country
metadata is provided by `countries-list` (MIT License).
